# Startup meter reference and requested pacing

Reference: local original EV 1.0.5 PowerPC executable (`/tmp/ev-ppc.txt`, regenerated by `tools/disassemble_ev_reference.py`) and CLUT 1000 in `Escape Velocity.rsrc`.

- `0x4acb0` initializes the meter. `0x4ad50..0x4ad78` selects indexed colors 247, 238 and 213. Original CLUT 1000 resolves these to RGB (8,235,0), (3,106,0) and (123,115,132).
- `0x4ada8..0x4adc4` sets a 200×10 rectangle centered horizontally, 45 pixels above the window bottom when its height exceeds 480. On the 832×624 design canvas this is (316,579,200,10). The short-window branch uses bottom−13 to bottom−3.
- `0x4aefc` updates the fill and clips its right edge to the meter. Insets and foreground-color changes establish the square-edged fill and border treatment.

The two-second logo hold and 1.5-second progressive loading presentation are user-requested modern pacing choices, not claims about original machine loading speed. A short completion hold makes the full bar visible. A setup delta is discarded on each startup screen; the progress presentation remains below completion until the game scene is ready. Added prompts and the loading caption are removed.
