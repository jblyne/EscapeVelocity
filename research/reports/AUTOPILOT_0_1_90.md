# Mission and commodity autopilot

Autopilot button (always available for cancellation outside title/death scenes) and reserved F6 shortcut open a classic picker. Clicking a row validates against current active mission/known trading options before engaging. Transient automation is canceled on pilot loading, death, pod ejection, title exit and manual steering/navigation. Pause and other service screens suspend it.

Transport jobs use next landing objective semantics from the mission interpreter, including return ports. Combat/boarding objectives must be completed manually before they appear. Ordinary connected BFS routing, gravity-well clearance, original jump sequencing, main-engine approach/braking, landing access checks and paid refueling are used. Mission reports remain open until Okay; automation then reevaluates progress. Refueling ports are selected if the current fuel cannot reach the next fuel-capable system on the route. Insufficient capacity, money, access or connection stops the route.

Trade pairs are restricted to discovered/surveyed adjacent systems with surviving accessible, inhabited exchange ports and low/high commodity bands. All six commodities are supported, including Luxury Goods. The picker quotes current per-tonne spread before fuel. The buying phase reserves 200 credits for two jump refills, requires expected spread to cover that cost, and uses free flagship/fleet cargo space. Sales are limited to cargo bought for the current automation run, preserving pre-existing same-commodity cargo. Prices and route access are revalidated at transaction time. Canceling keeps purchased cargo aboard for manual handling; automation does not resume automatically after loading a saved pilot.

Validation: 30 focused checks exercise an actual courier flight/landing, original completion-report handoff, two complete Luxury Goods trading loops, net earnings, protected pre-existing cargo, adjacent low/high discovery filters, insufficient-fuel stops, destruction, cancellation of an active jump, and reopening selection. Also passed 115 base gameplay, 16 controls/strict, 22 jump audio, and 7 auto-landing checks. Picker layouts were rendered and visually inspected.
