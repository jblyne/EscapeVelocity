# Combat AI and timing follow-up — 0.1.155

## Evidence and corrected mismatches

The recovered weapon records supply Reload, Count, Speed, Guidance and mount/loadout fields. The local original manual at lines 589–615 establishes simultaneous primary weapons and turret refusal without a valid target. The remake previously used an extra laser lifetime of 1.5 seconds, a universal NPC firing distance of 950, and NPC reload divided by up to four mounts with a minimum of 0.12 seconds. Those three prototype overrides are removed. Count and Reload now share the remake's existing 60 Hz weapon clock; that clock conversion is not newly certified against measured executable cadence.

NPCs fire simultaneous mount volleys at the unmodified per-type reload period. Finite ammo decrements per successful projectile. Fractional time carries across frame boundaries, with a bounded eight-volley catch-up per update and global projectile limits. Range-gated idle time cannot accumulate overdue fire. Projectile weapons use speed times actual lifetime for AI range; bays and beams retain existing reconstructed ranges. The requested custom Maverick values are preserved.

Turrets require selected/designated contacts; rear turrets reject targets outside their aft hemisphere. NPC main engines use EVFlight.step rather than moving the velocity vector toward a desired vector. Attack runs estimate target motion using projectile speed, and close according to primary-weapon reach. The intercept estimate, throttle alignment threshold and engagement offset are reconstruction choices, not source-certified original AI algorithms.

## Additional executable investigation

Read-only reference: original EV 1.0.5 PPC disassembly /tmp/ev-ppc.txt generated by the repository tools. The inspection branch at 0x31e4–0x325c computes per-axis relative velocities and rejects magnitudes at or above TOC -0x6b50, before the scan call at 0x339c. Decoding data section offset 0x8000-0x6b50 yields double 0.35; TOC -0x6b18 still yields the verified 180.0 per-axis position boundary. A safe conversion between original runtime velocity units and Godot world units remains unverified. Therefore the existing five-second scan polling is not changed or relabeled as exact original behavior.

## Remaining fidelity limits

Original acceleration/turning unit conversions, exact scan acquisition cadence, retreat decision thresholds, ambient-arrival timing, and exhaustive manual campaign comparisons remain open. This update addresses concrete combat mismatches without inventing authoritative constants for those systems. Legacy add-ons remain excluded.
