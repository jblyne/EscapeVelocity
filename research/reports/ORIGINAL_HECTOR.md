# Cap’n Hector cameo

The recovered original EV Data character record (`përs` 639, decoded.json) names Cap’n Hector and specifies ShipType 136. The catalog identifies ship 136 as Rapier. Its original jet-like silhouette is already represented by the reconstructed Rapier model.

The community history at https://evn.fandom.com/wiki/Hector corroborates the original Rapier and periodic registration reminders. The recovered manual describes the original shareware registration system, but does not establish exact encounter timing.

This implementation is a nostalgic reminder-only cameo: first visit after 180–360 seconds of ordinary flight; later cooldowns 360–600 seconds; a 30-second visit and ten-second main-screen broadcast. These timings and the dialogue are authored for this reconstruction. Docking, menus, and hyperspace do not accelerate its schedule. There is no payment workflow, registration lockout, credit theft, or trial-period attack escalation. The original special character’s immunity is retained without reputation penalties.
